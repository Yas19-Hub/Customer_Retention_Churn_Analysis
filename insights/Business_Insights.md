# Business Insights

## 1. Customer Churn
- Overall churn rate is 22%, indicating room to improve customer retention strategies.

## 2. Revenue
- Enterprise plan generates the highest Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR).

## 3. Customer Distribution
- DevTools and FinTech represent the largest customer segments.

## 4. Churn Reasons
- Feature-related issues are the leading cause of customer churn.

## 5. Support Performance
- Support tickets are distributed across all priority levels, indicating a balanced workload.

## 6. Product Usage
- A small number of features account for the majority of platform usage.

## 7. Recommendation
- Improve high-impact features and proactively support customers on plans with higher churn to reduce customer loss.
